Voice Teleprompter + Camera

Paste your script and press Start. Your camera fills the page and the script sits in a box at the top, right under the camera lens, so your eyes stay close to the camera while you read. Your voice and video are recorded.

Tip for eye contact: a narrow script width and 2 or 3 lines keep your eyes almost still, so viewers can't tell you are reading.
